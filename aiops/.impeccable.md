## Design Context

### Users
Mike operates his own websites and APIs on Mini and Sky. He needs to understand
incidents quickly, review recovery evidence and control automated actions.

### Brand Personality
Calm, compact, dependable. The interface should make uncertainty and action status
clear without amplifying anxiety during an outage.

### Aesthetic Direction
The user selected a calm, compact operations console with light and dark themes.
Use restrained slate and warm neutral surfaces, clear typography, and semantic
status colours paired with labels. Avoid decorative charts and oversized metrics.

### Design Principles
- Put current incidents and approval requests first.
- Distinguish observations, hypotheses and verified outcomes.
- Keep controls accessible on phones and keyboards.
- Show failed requests and stale data explicitly.
- Confirm consequential actions with their exact target and scope.
